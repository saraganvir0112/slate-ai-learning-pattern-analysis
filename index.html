INPUTS

Five signals, read continuously

No single test defines a learner. Slate blends day-to-day signal instead of waiting for the next exam.

Attendance rhythm

Not just present/absent — arrival timing and consistency across weeks.

Quiz & test scores

Trend across assessments, weighted more toward recent performance.

Submission timing

How close to the deadline work lands — an early signal of load or disengagement.

Time on task

Minutes actually spent in lessons and practice sets, not just logins.

Engagement depth

Forum posts, question-asking, and voluntary practice beyond what's assigned.

CLUSTERING

Four ways students tend to move

Slate groups learners by trajectory shape, not just current grade — two students with the same score today can be headed in very different directions.

Steady climbers

Consistent upward trend across most signals. Low intervention priority.

Late bloomers

Slow start, accelerating engagement. Watch for a delayed grade catch-up.

Plateaued

Flat trend after early progress — often a sign material has outpaced pace.

At-risk decliners

Falling attendance and scores together. Highest intervention priority.

UNDER THE HOOD

How a prediction gets made

Four stages, running continuously through the term rather than once at the end.

01

Collect

Pull attendance, gradebook, LMS activity, and submission logs each day.

02

Detect

Cluster each learner's trajectory shape against known patterns.

03

Predict

Project the term-end outcome with a confidence range, updated weekly.

04

Alert

Flag risk shifts to teachers with a plain-language reason, not just a score.
